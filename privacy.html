Overview

ImagorAI is a free, client-side tool. This version does not require an account, does not use a database, and does not store the prompts you generate on any server.

Information We Collect

ImagorAI does not collect personal information beyond standard, anonymized website analytics that may be enabled in the future (such as page views, general location by country, or device type) to help us understand how the site is used. Any idea, text or selection you enter into the generator stays in your browser and is never transmitted to or stored on a server.

Cookies

This version of ImagorAI stores your language preference (English or Portuguese) locally in your browser so it's remembered on your next visit. It does not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

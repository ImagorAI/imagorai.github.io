About ImagorAI

Turning simple ideas into detailed AI image prompts.

A free, bilingual tool built for anyone who wants better AI images without learning prompt engineering.

Most AI image tools produce their best results when you feed them a detailed, well-structured prompt — the right style, lighting, mood, camera and composition, all described precisely. Getting there usually means learning prompt engineering by trial and error.

ImagorAI removes that barrier. Type a simple idea, or pick from a set of curated options, and ImagorAI assembles a polished, ready-to-use prompt in seconds — one you can paste straight into your favorite AI image generator.

01

Start with an idea

Write your own idea in a sentence, or choose from subject, style, lighting, mood, location and camera options.

02

Generate instantly

ImagorAI builds a detailed, natural-language prompt in your browser — no account, no waiting, no AI API required.

03

Copy and create

Copy the prompt and paste it into the AI image generator you already use — Midjourney, ChatGPT, Gemini and more.

This first version runs entirely client-side and is completely free to use. We're building it as the foundation for a small toolkit of specialized generators — headshots, travel photography, cinematic scenes, product shots and more — so it's worth checking back as ImagorAI grows.
